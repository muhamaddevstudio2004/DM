      How It Works
      

      1. Pick
      Choose a free locker from the ten available.
      2. Fill
      Add files and notes.
      

      3. Lock
      Set a password. The 24 hour timer starts.
      4. Open

      5. Expire
      After 24 hours everything is deleted and the locker is free again.
